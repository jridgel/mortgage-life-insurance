Coverage Options

We shop top-rated carriers to find you the best rate. Here's what we typically recommend for new homeowners.

Term Life

Most Popular

Pure death benefit protection for a set term — 10, 15, 20, or 30 years. Perfectly matches your mortgage duration.

  • Lowest monthly premiums
  • Match your mortgage term exactly
  • Level premiums — never go up
  • Tax-free death benefit
  • Optional living benefit riders
Get Term Quote

Whole Life

Lifelong

Permanent coverage that builds cash value over time. Premiums are fixed for life and the policy never expires.

  • Coverage never expires
  • Builds cash value you can borrow
  • Guaranteed level premiums
  • Dividend potential (mutual carriers)
  • Estate planning tool
Get Whole Life Quote

Universal Life

Flexible

Flexible permanent coverage where you can adjust your premium and death benefit as your needs change.

  • Adjustable premium payments
  • Flexible death benefit
  • Cash value accumulation
  • Indexed growth options (IUL)
  • Long-term care riders available
Get UL Quote
